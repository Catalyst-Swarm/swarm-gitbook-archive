# Chapter 1 | Part 1 - Defining Goals

**What specific goals does the organization want to achieve, and how do they align with its overall mission?**

As a young project or enterprise, it's important to have clear goals that are aligned with your organization's overall mission. This will help you focus your efforts and resources towards achieving the desired outcomes.

For example, if your organization's mission is to provide affordable and eco-friendly housing to low-income families, your goals may include increasing the number of housing units, reducing construction costs, and improving energy efficiency.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What is the organization's overall mission?
2. What specific goals has the organization set for itself?
3. Are these goals specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART)?
4. How do these goals align with the organization's mission and values?
5. What resources are needed to achieve these goals?
6. How will progress towards these goals be measured and tracked?
7. Are there any potential obstacles to achieving these goals?
8. How can these obstacles be overcome?
9. How will achieving these goals benefit the organization and its stakeholders?
10. Are these goals regularly reviewed and updated to ensure they remain relevant and achievable?

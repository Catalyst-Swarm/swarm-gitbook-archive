# Chapter 1 | Part 2 - Employee Motivation

**How can the organization motivate and attract employees who share its values and mission?**

Your organization's employees are a crucial factor in achieving success. To build a strong team, it's important to attract and motivate employees who share your organization's values and mission. This may involve offering competitive salaries and benefits, creating a positive work environment, and providing opportunities for professional development.

For example, if your organization's mission is to reduce food waste, you may look for employees who are passionate about sustainability and food justice.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What are the organization's values and mission?
2. What type of employees would be a good fit for the organization?
3. How can the organization attract and retain employees who share its values and mission?
4. What type of compensation and benefits package is needed to attract top talent?
5. How can the organization create a positive work environment that fosters collaboration, innovation, and employee engagement?
6. What type of professional development opportunities can be offered to employees to help them grow and develop within the organization?
7. How can the organization ensure that employees feel valued and recognized for their contributions?
8. Are there any potential obstacles to attracting and retaining top talent?
9. How can these obstacles be addressed?
10. How can the organization continuously improve its employee attraction and retention strategies?

# Chapter 2 | Part 2 - Principles and Guidelines

**How can leaders communicate and reinforce these values to employees and stakeholders?** <br>

As leaders, it's important to communicate your organization's core values clearly and consistently to employees and stakeholders. This may involve using various communication channels, such as company meetings, newsletters, or social media. Additionally, it's important to reinforce these values through actions, such as recognizing employees who embody them or making decisions that align with them.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What are the organization's core values?
2. How can leaders communicate these core values clearly and consistently to employees and stakeholders?
3. What communication channels can be used to reinforce these values (e.g., company meetings, public events, newsletters, social media)?
4. How can leaders ensure that employees and stakeholders understand the importance of these core values?
5. How can leaders model these core values in their own behavior and decision-making?
6. How can leaders recognize and reward employees who embody these core values?
7. How can leaders ensure that policies and practices align with these core values?
8. How can leaders respond when core values are not being upheld?
9. How can leaders create a culture of accountability around these core values?
10. How can leaders continuously monitor and reinforce these core values to ensure they remain a priority for the organization?

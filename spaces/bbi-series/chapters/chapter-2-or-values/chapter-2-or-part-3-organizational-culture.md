# Chapter 2 | Part 3 - Organizational Culture

**What kind of culture is needed to achieve the organization's mission, and what steps can be taken to build it?** <br>

The culture of your organization can have a significant impact on its ability to achieve its mission. For example, if your organization's mission is to improve public health, a culture of collaboration, innovation, and accountability may be necessary to drive success.&#x20;

To build this culture, you may need to establish clear expectations, foster open communication, and provide resources and support for employee growth and development.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What is the organization's mission?
2. What type of culture is needed to achieve this mission?
3. How can this culture be communicated to employees and stakeholders?
4. How can leaders model and reinforce this culture?
5. What policies and practices are needed to support this culture?
6. How can the organization foster open communication and collaboration among employees?
7. How can the organization encourage innovation and experimentation?
8. How can the organization recognize and reward employees who embody this culture?
9. What type of resources and support can be provided to help employees grow and develop within this culture?
10. How can the organization continuously monitor and improve its culture to ensure it remains aligned with its mission?

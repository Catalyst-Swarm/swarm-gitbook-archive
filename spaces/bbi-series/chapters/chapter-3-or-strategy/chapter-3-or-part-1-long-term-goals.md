# Chapter 3 | Part 1 - Long Term Goals

**What specific goals does the organization want to achieve in the next 5 or 10 years, and how will it measure success?** <br>

To achieve long-term success, it's important to set specific, measurable goals that align with your organization's mission and values. These goals may include increasing revenue, expanding into new markets, or launching new products or services. To measure success, you may need to establish key performance indicators (KPIs) and regularly track progress towards these goals.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What is the organization's mission and vision for the future?
2. What specific goals does the organization want to achieve in the next 5 or 10 years?
3. Why are these goals important for the organization's success?
4. How do these goals align with the organization's mission and values?
5. What key performance indicators (KPIs) can be used to measure progress towards these goals?
6. How frequently should progress be tracked and reported?
7. What resources and support will be needed to achieve these goals?
8. How can the organization ensure that employees and stakeholders are aware of these goals?
9. How can the organization adjust its strategy if progress towards these goals is not being made?
10. How can the organization continuously improve its goal-setting and measurement processes to drive success in the future?

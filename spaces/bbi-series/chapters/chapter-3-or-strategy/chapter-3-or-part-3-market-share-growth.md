# Chapter 3 | Part 3 - Market Share Growth

**What is the organization's plan for expanding its market share, and what resources will it need to achieve this?**

Expanding market share can be a key driver of growth for your organization. To achieve this, you may need to develop a strategic plan that outlines how you will increase your customer base and outpace competitors. This may involve conducting market research, developing marketing campaigns, or investing in new product development. To support these efforts, you may need to allocate resources and build a strong team.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What is the organization's current market position?
2. What are the organization's long-term goals for market share expansion?
3. Why is market share expansion important for the organization's success?
4. What strategies will be used to increase market share?
5. How will the organization conduct market research to inform its expansion plan?
6. What new products or services will be developed to support market share expansion?
7. What marketing campaigns will be used to reach new customers?
8. How will the organization measure the success of its market share expansion efforts?
9. What resources and support will be needed to achieve market share expansion?
10. How can the organization continuously monitor and adjust its market share expansion plan to ensure success in the future?

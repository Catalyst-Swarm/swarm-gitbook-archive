Financial planning is critical to the success of any organization. In this chapter, we'll examine how to maximize profits while also making a positive social impact. We'll explore different business models for selling, how to target returns for shareholders, and how to measure the social impact of your organization.&#x20;

We'll discuss questions like what marginal return do we want to maintain, what type of social impact do we want to have, what is our primary business model for selling, and what should we target for return for our shareholders?&#x20;

By the end of this chapter, you'll have a clear understanding of your organization's financial goals and strategies for achieving them while also making a positive impact.



**Chapter 5 | Part 1 (** [**view page**](chapter-5-or-part-1-target-marginal-return.md) **)**

* How does the organization define marginal return, and how does it factor into decision-making?&#x20;
* (Time Rating: 6)

To ensure profitability, it's important to establish a target marginal return for all product offerings. This may involve analyzing costs, pricing strategies, and market demand to determine the appropriate profit margin. Once a target is established, you can develop strategies to achieve it, such as cost-cutting measures or targeted marketing campaigns.

**Chapter 5 | Part 2 (** [**view page**](chapter-5-or-part-2-social-impact.md) **)**

* What kind of social impact does the organization want to have, and how can it achieve it while still maintaining profitability?&#x20;
* (Time Rating: 6)

Many organizations today are seeking to make a positive social impact in addition to generating profits. To achieve this, you may need to identify a specific social issue or cause that aligns with your organization's mission and values. Once this is identified, you can develop strategies to measure and track your organization's impact, such as tracking volunteer hours, donating a percentage of profits to charity, or conducting an annual social impact report.

<br>

**Chapter 5 | Part 3 (** [**view page**](chapter-5-or-part-3-business-model.md) **)**

* Which business model (B2B, B2C, B2B2C) is most appropriate for the organization, and why?&#x20;
* (Time Rating: 7)

The choice of business model can have a significant impact on an organization's ability to generate revenue and stay competitive. To identify the most appropriate business model, you may need to consider factors such as customer behavior, market demand, and pricing. Additionally, it's important to be aware of changes in the market and adapt your business model accordingly, such as shifting from a B2B to a B2C model or offering subscription-based services.

<br>

**Chapter 5 | Part 4 (** [**view page**](chapter-5-or-part-4-shareholder-return.md) **)**

* What is the target return for shareholders, and how does it align with the organization's overall goals?&#x20;
* (Time Rating: 6)

To achieve this, you may need to establish a target return on investment and develop strategies to achieve it. This may involve analyzing financial data, identifying areas for growth, or diversifying revenue streams. Additionally, it's important to balance the needs of shareholders with other stakeholders, such as employees, customers, and the broader community.

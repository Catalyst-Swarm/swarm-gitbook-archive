# Chapter 5 | Part 1 - Target Marginal Return

**How does the organization define marginal return, and how does it factor into decision-making?**&#x20;

To ensure profitability, it's important to establish a target marginal return for all product offerings. This may involve analyzing costs, pricing strategies, and market demand to determine the appropriate profit margin. Once a target is established, you can develop strategies to achieve it, such as cost-cutting measures or targeted marketing campaigns.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. How does the organization define marginal return, and what factors are considered in its calculation?
2. What is the process for establishing a target marginal return for each product offering, and how do costs, pricing strategies, and market demand factor into this process?
3. How do the organization's marginal returns compare to industry benchmarks or competitors' performance?
4. In what ways does the organization's marginal return factor into its overall decision-making process?
5. What strategies does the organization implement to achieve its target marginal returns, such as cost-cutting measures or targeted marketing campaigns?
6. How does the organization monitor and adjust its product offerings' marginal returns to ensure that they remain profitable and aligned with market conditions?
7. What tools or techniques does the organization use to analyze and optimize its pricing strategies to achieve the desired marginal returns?
8. How do changes in market demand or competitive landscape impact the organization's target marginal returns, and how are these changes addressed?
9. How does the organization balance the pursuit of marginal returns with other key performance indicators, such as customer satisfaction or market share?
10. What challenges or obstacles might the organization face in achieving its target marginal returns, and how can they be addressed or mitigated?

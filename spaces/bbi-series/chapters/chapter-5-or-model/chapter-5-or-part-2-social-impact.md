# Chapter 5 | Part 2 - Social Impact

**What kind of social impact does the organization want to have, and how can it achieve it while still maintaining profitability?**&#x20;

Many organizations today are seeking to make a positive social impact in addition to generating profits. To achieve this, you may need to identify a specific social issue or cause that aligns with your organization's mission and values. Once this is identified, you can develop strategies to measure and track your organization's impact, such as tracking volunteer hours, donating a percentage of profits to charity, or conducting an annual social impact report.



Here some guiding question which can help you finding the right answers:

1. What specific social issues or causes does the organization want to address, and how do they align with its mission and values?
2. How does the organization balance its desire for social impact with the need to maintain profitability and financial stability?
3. What strategies or initiatives has the organization implemented to make a positive social impact, such as volunteer programs, charitable donations, or sustainable practices?
4. How does the organization measure and track its social impact, and what tools or metrics are used for this purpose?
5. Are there any industry standards or benchmarks that the organization can use to evaluate its social impact performance compared to competitors or peers?
6. How can the organization effectively communicate its social impact goals and achievements to stakeholders, including customers, employees, and investors?
7. What challenges or obstacles might the organization face in pursuing its social impact objectives while maintaining profitability, and how can they be addressed or mitigated?
8. How can the organization continuously improve and expand its social impact initiatives while staying aligned with its core business objectives?
9. Are there any partnerships or collaborations the organization can explore to increase its social impact or share best practices with other organizations?
10. How does the organization ensure that its commitment to social impact remains a priority in decision-making processes and long-term strategic planning?

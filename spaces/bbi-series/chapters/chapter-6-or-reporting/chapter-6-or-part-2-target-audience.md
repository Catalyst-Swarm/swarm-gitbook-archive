# Chapter 6 | Part 2 - Target Audience

**Who is the target audience for the report, and what are their needs and expectations?**&#x20;

To ensure that the report is effective, it's important to tailor the information and presentation style to the target audience. For example, if the report is intended for investors, it may need to include financial data, market analysis, and growth projections presented in a clear and concise manner. If the report is intended for employees, it may need to include information about the organization's mission, values, and culture presented in an engaging and informative way

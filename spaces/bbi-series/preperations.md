Hey there.

Before starting the exercise, you can benefit from setting up some basic preperations and operational framework.

1. **Begin with an overview**: Start by briefly explaining the importance of defining clear organizational goals and how they contribute to the overall success and direction of the organization.
2. **Brainstorming sessions**: Organize timely online brainstorming sessions with key stakeholders, including Team members, consultants, friends or collaborators and community members, to gather diverse perspectives and ideas about what the organization should aim to achieve.
3. **Identify themes**: Analyze the ideas generated during the brainstorming session and group them into common themes or categories. This will help create a clearer picture of the organization's potential goals.
4. **Set SMART goals**: Transform the themes into Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART) goals. This ensures that the goals are well-defined, realistic, and trackable.
5. **Prioritize goals**: Facilitate async discussions to prioritize the goals based on their importance and urgency. This will help the organization focus its resources on the most crucial objectives.
6. **Break down goals into objectives and tasks**: For each prioritized goal, break it down into smaller, manageable objectives and tasks. This will make the goals more actionable and easier to work towards.
7. **Assign responsibility and deadlines**: Ensure that each objective and task has a designated person or team responsible for its completion, along with a deadline. This promotes accountability and ensures progress is made towards achieving the goals.
8. **Monitor and evaluate progress**: Establish a system for regularly monitoring progress towards the goals, including key performance indicators (KPIs) and milestones. This will help identify any obstacles or challenges and make necessary adjustments to stay on track.
9. **Celebrate achievements**: Recognize and celebrate the achievements of the organization and its employees as milestones are reached and goals are accomplished. This will boost morale and reinforce the importance of working towards the defined goals.
10. **Review and revise goals periodically**: Conduct regular reviews of the organization's goals to ensure they remain relevant and aligned with the changing business environment. Adjust goals as needed and repeat the process to continue driving the organization's growth and success.

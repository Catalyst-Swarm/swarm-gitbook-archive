## Strategic Planning Exercise

Defining a business model is one of the most critical steps for any young project or enterprise looking to achieve success. Developing a comprehensive business strategy is essential to help founders identify their organization's purpose, values, target customers, and unique value proposition.

In this guide, we've outlined a series of questions and structures that young projects can use to develop a solid business strategy. By answering these questions and following these structures, founders can gain a clear understanding of their organization's goals, strengths, weaknesses, and the steps needed to achieve success.

Whether you're just starting out or looking to refine your existing business strategy, this guide can help you build a strong foundation for your project or enterprise.

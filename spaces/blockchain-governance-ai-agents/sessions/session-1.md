## Meeting Summary

The meeting focused on the role of AI in governance, with discussions on liquid democracy, on-chain governance, and the potential impact and challenges of AI agents. The conversation also explored the decentralization of AI resources and the ethical considerations surrounding AI governance within decentralized systems. The section overall shed light on the complexities and challenges associated with integrating AI into decision-making processes.

The meeting also discussed the diverse roles and interactions of autonomous agents in the context of AI governance, including the potential use of AI to predict governance votes and the inherent problems of traditional voting systems. The section focused on the ethical considerations and crisis resolution aspects of AI governance, emphasizing the need for consent-based decision-making to establish a stronger ethical foundation for AI-human interactions. The meeting concluded with a proposal to establish biweekly or monthly calls to continue the conversation, with discussions on the potential role of AI in decision-making processes within the group.



### Chapters & Topics:

#### Introduction and Background Information

Steph introduces the meeting by providing background information on the participants and their connections to the topic of AI and Blockchain governance. The discussion focuses on the potential impact of AI agents in liquid democracy and on-chain governance, with examples of projects and individuals involved in these areas. The meeting aims to bring everyone up to speed on the topic and set the direction for the conversation.

\* AI and governance\
\* Liquid democracy and on-chain governance

#### Discussion on AI, Governance, and Decentralization

On the innovative use of AI in governance, highlighting the importance of human understanding and responsibility in AI-powered systems. The discussion also goes into the decentralization of AI resources and the necessity for ethical AI governance within decentralized systems, with concerns raised about the potential impact of large language models on governance processes.

\* Decentralization of AI resources\
\* Role of AI agents in governance

#### Discussion on Agent Swarms and Autonomous Agents

On the concept of agent swarms, where multiple agents collaborate as a larger organism, rather than a single AI performing all tasks and about the potential roles of different AI agents, including treasury growth, sustainability, onboarding tactics, and governance functions. The discussion also emphasizes the importance of integrity and trust in the roles of autonomous agents, highlighting the need for credentialing and declaring their roles.

#### Ethical Considerations and Crisis Resolution in AI Governance

Concerns about the accuracy and value of using AI to predict the outcome of governance votes. Highlighting the flaws in traditional voting systems and advocates for consent-based decision-making to establish a stronger ethical foundation for AI-human interactions and emphasizing the need for adaptability and crisis resolution in AI governance.

\* Crisis resolution in governance



#### Key Questions:

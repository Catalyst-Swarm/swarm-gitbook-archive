# Cardano Governance Office Hours

The Cardano Governance - Open Office Hour sessions are public community events which take place on a weekly schedule on X (previously twitter). The event provides an open space for the Cardano Community to address questions and ideas on everything governance as well as provide latest updates and announcements on relevant initiatives around Cardano Governance.

The sesions take place each tuesday on X \
To join the sessions, chack Nicolas Cerny Twitter account: [https://twitter.com/NicolasC3rny](https://twitter.com/NicolasC3rny)&#x20;



This Gitbook aims to document and archive the Open Office hours so that we keep notes and reflections and ideally improve some part of the Cardano Knowledge Management on all things Governance.

This documentation is a Cardano Community initiative by SWARM and we do it because it simply matters :)

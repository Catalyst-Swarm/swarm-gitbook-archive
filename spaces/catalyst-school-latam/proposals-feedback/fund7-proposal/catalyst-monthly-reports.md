#### answer:

Yes.

Considering: i) the "time slot" issue in catalyst ecosystem for promoting recurring workshops; ii) low attendance in workshops, but good numbers in views on youtube \[the-catalyst-school youtube channel as example https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCIPvRvMoxhmHLUuPPcsMmmg]; the team decided to convert the workshop (as we know), in interviews to try a different approach on the onboarding front, as well as covering all the topics of that particular interview \[example: if the topics is about "intro-to-catalyst", the interview should cover all topics like a)whats a CA?; b)whats a vCA; c) the Catalyst Process; et cetera]. In addition to the interviews, there are all the supporting materials (translated from the-catalyst-school, from IOG and swarm), in different formats as: videos, presentations, podcasts and documentation in both portuguese and Spanish languages.

&#x20;

The team also noticed a low interaction of portuguese speaker in the LATAM community as whole (as seen in latam townhall videos, available at: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCRZKx4HJFifOy8HCeOB8pxw)\
So, the team is working together with LATAM townhall team to find a solution for this.

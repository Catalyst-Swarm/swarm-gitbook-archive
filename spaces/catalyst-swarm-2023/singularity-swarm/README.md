# Singularity Swarm

The Catalyst Swarm successfully submitted a proposal in Project Catalyst Fund 9 and have been approved to receive 60400 Usd in Ada to work on the collaboration between the Catalyst Swarm & SingularityNET.

Proposal link: [https://cardano.ideascale.com/c/idea/421782](https://cardano.ideascale.com/c/idea/421782)&#x20;

In October 2022, Swarm received first funds from Project Catalyst to ensure further developments and improvements of our collaborations from Swarm to the SingularityNET ecosystem.

Till date (January 2023 ) we received 37750 Usd in Ada from the total requested 60400 Usd in Ada

These Fund have been used so far to:

* Introduce Deep Fund Experiment and Governance discussion to Cardano Community and incentivising collaboration with focus on Deep Funding Governance Expert Group
* Establish SingularityNET dedicated support Wallet & Fund at Catalyst Swarm [https://pool.pm/addr1qycauahztlv3mlulgrmp95kvn2mcfdaaqqeq89dft6vhjs8h2z5ksqjg8jwcx3m2sl70h35tm9cm7w7ksue3mhufetyql0lw53](https://pool.pm/addr1qycauahztlv3mlulgrmp95kvn2mcfdaaqqeq89dft6vhjs8h2z5ksqjg8jwcx3m2sl70h35tm9cm7w7ksue3mhufetyql0lw53)&#x20;
* Financially Support the SingularityNET Community Podcast which is part of the SingularityNET Ambassador program
* Launch Bounties for the SingularityNET Community\
  2 Established boards & Spaces with 160 tracked tasks\
  [https://app.dework.xyz/catalyst-swarm/singularitynet-and-swa](https://app.dework.xyz/catalyst-swarm/singularitynet-and-swa) \
  [https://app.dework.xyz/singularitynet-ambas](https://app.dework.xyz/singularitynet-ambas)\
  [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1f\_wzxgOAi\_77rGEJwCoFDYZunQHn-\_hYpVH3qUDsnOM/edit?usp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1f_wzxgOAi_77rGEJwCoFDYZunQHn-_hYpVH3qUDsnOM/edit?usp=sharing)
* Enable time and resources from the Swarm Treasury Team to implement AGIX token to the Swarm Treasury & Accounting system & develop easy Token integrations to the Treasury systems
* Supporting & initiating a wide range of SingularityNET & Deep Funding community events, sessions and workshops

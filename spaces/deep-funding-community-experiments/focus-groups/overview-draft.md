# Overview (DRAFT)

Deep Funding Focus groups aim to facilitate co-working and communication between SingularityNET and the community.

## What is a focus group ?

A Deep Funding focus group is a small group of people (3 to 4) who work on specific subjects as a service for SingularityNET and the community. A critical friend. Community researchers.



## What do we address ?&#x20;

Focus Groups do not aim to make decisions but cultivate and refine common understanding in order to communicate and engage with a wider community.&#x20;

### 1 - Common understanding

What is the common understanding ?

### 2 - Resolving misunderstanding

Identify any ambiguities, misunderstanding or contradictions. Shared vocabularies.

### 3 - Refining understanding

Refine and incorporate understanding

### 4 - Communicating to the wider community

Consider how to communicate to wider community

The DF focus group helps communicate the issues to the community making sure everyone is on the same page and agrees about what the context is. Addressing why questions are being asked, even if they don’t agree on the answers, Establishing shared vocabularies etc. There is an educational use for this but that’s a spin-off not the primary purpose.

### 5 - Engaging with community queries

Incorporating community questions and responses

Facilitating people own judgement by providing information and context.

What is going to be done with the information, how is it going to be used. What is acceptance criteria ?

### 6 - Incorporating community feedback

How to assess and incorporate community feedback&#x20;

Collection of baseline data or understanding

# Deep-Funding-Community-Experiments (Draft)

## What is a community experiment ?

### A process of change

What distinguishes a community experiment is open participation in a process of change.

{% hint style="info" %}
"_The path to a fully decentralized Foundation has undergone some change, adapting to the current realities of the crypto ecosystem and the learnings we have gathered along the way_." - An evolving approach, SingComDAO Initiation Document
{% endhint %}

### Contingent organization

The process of change feeds back into the community making it a "_living organisation_" that is dynamic, liquid and contingent.

{% hint style="info" %}
"_... a document like this one is time-bound and liable to change according to new insights and future decisions._" - A living organization, SingComDAO Initiation Document
{% endhint %}

## Common characteristics of an experiment

### A Hypothesis

Experiments are "theory laden" in the sense that they test a theory, a conjecture or a hypothesis.

### Experimental group

The experimental group or subject is the scope of what is being tested. This should specify what is to be changed or maniplulated and what variables are to be included.

### Control group

The control group, condition or subject remains unchanged or receives a standard treatment for comparison purposes.

### Randomization

The process of randomly assigning participants or subjects to different groups to minimize bias.&#x20;

### Data collection and documentation

The systematic gathering and documentation of measurements, observations, or data related to the dependent variables.&#x20;

### Analysis

The statistical or qualitative examination of the collected data to draw conclusions and make inferences.

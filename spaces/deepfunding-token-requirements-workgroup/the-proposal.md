## Overview

Photrek and NFT Guild's proposal aims to develop requirements for a DeepFunding Token that goes beyond the limitations of AGIX, creating a multifaceted governance system within Deep Funding and SingularityNET.&#x20;

This initiative seeks to represent social incentives such as engagement and participation through the token, enhancing community-driven dynamics. The proposal includes workshops, both open community and expert discussions, to ideate and design the token.&#x20;

The outcome will be a recommendation document presented to the SingularityNET Foundation and DeepFunding Team. With a budget of $5,000 in AGIX, the proposal aims to establish a resource pool and expertise to guide the token's design beyond the initial project phase.

## Summary

Photrek and NFT Token propose to develop the requirements for a DeepFunding Token within both the Deep Funding ecosystem and the broader SingularityNET network. This is central to creating a more multifaceted, transparent, and decentralized governance system for Deep Funding.&#x20;

By moving beyond the limitations of only utilizing AGIX and emphasizing the comprehensive representation of social incentives such as user engagement and participation, the proposal is another step in fostering a more dynamic and inclusive community-driven ecosystem within Deep Funding and SingularityNET.

## The problem we are aiming to solve

In today's governance system of Deep Funding, AGIX is the only token that is being utilized. While it serves as a critical part of the deep funding process, AGIX alone cannot capture everything that's required to build a fully decentralized governance process. This limitation poses a challenge in creating a dynamic and multifaceted system. Furthermore, there's a pressing need to distinguish between financial value, represented by AGIX holdings, and social value, which encompasses community engagement, participation, and contributions. Such a distinction is paramount for a holistic and balanced governance structure.

The Deep Fund voting system uses a unique configuration that considers a user's engagement score with the square root of their AGIX balance. Photrek has built a proof of concept and is proposing to build a minimum viable product to automate the process of computing engagement scores.. However, an existing challenge remains: transparently reflecting and representing these calculated outputs on-chain. This limitation restricts the opportunities that such an application could provide for both deep funding and the broader SNET and Cardano ecosystem.

This initiative is a follow up to the submitted DeepFunding token proposal in the previous Governance Round.

## Our solution

Photrek and NFT Guild propose to research the requirements for a Deep Fund New Project that will examine the options and opportunities for storing DeepFunding engagement metrics on-chain. The output of the project will be a high-quality proposal with supporting research documents.

Deep Funding has acknowledged the importance of community voice. To this end, social incentives such as engagement and participation are being utilized to amplify the community's say by capturing and incorporating various factors into their voting weight with various efforts underway to automate these processes.

Yet, the current system lacks a mechanism to properly reflect this complex data. The proposed solution is a DeepFunding Token, designed to reflect these configured scores on-chain, serving and introducing various utilities within Deep Funding and eventually across the entire SingularityNET ecosystem. This token symbolizes a more comprehensive solution that caters to the multifaceted needs of decentralized governance.We will use the Cardano Blockchain and use a Cardano native asset as DeepFunding Token.

Introducing Phase One - Ideation and Design: This proposal represents the first phase, focused on ideation and design. Collaboration with experts and community members is essential. Two workshops will be held to gather insights, questions and feedback, which will culminate in a detailed recommendation document. This document will form the basis for a New Project proposal for deep funding Round 4, covering how to create the DF token and what will be needed for the project's continuation.

The final document, encompassing all inputs, will be presented as a recommendation to the SingularityNET Foundation and the DeepFunding Team. The Principal Investigator, Juana Attieh, Photrek Project Manager, will guide the entirety of the project, ensuring alignment and continuation with the broader goals. Furthermore, this initiative aims to establish a first initial resource pool which provides all needed knowledge and skill set to guide the DeepFunding token design process beyond the time frame of this proposal. The collaborating groups, including Photrek and the NFT Guild will develop hands-on experience and expertise to maintain further developments.

The workshops will focus on a range of critical topics such as utility, future possibilities, legalities, resources, and building requirements. These sessions will enable stakeholders and community members to dive deep into the complexities and nuances of the proposal, laying a strong foundation for successful implementation.

We plan to host two open community workshops and the team will follow-up with expert discussions..&#x20;

### Open Community Workshops

These Workshops will be public and open community events, inviting everyone interested in joining the events and to participate in specific topics and themes around the DF Token Ideation. Main objective of these workshops is to generate community driven innovation and perspectives, ensuring that voices are heard and included in further steps. Each Workshop will take place in a time frame of 1hr, starting with a short introduction and context, then following up with open discussions with participants.

### Team & Expert Discussions

In these discussions, the team will meet and invite specific experts and thought-leaders in tokenomics and blockchain technology, ensuring that beforehand gathered community perspectives are well reflected and included in a feasible and effective manner.<br>

All workshops will be held on Zoom and will be recorded. Recordings will be made public to the Community, ensuring a maximum of transparency of the DF Token Ideation. Each Workshop will focus on a specific topic around the DF Token, making sure that the Workshops stay focused and in scope.

# Project Overview

Start Date: January 2024\
End Date: February 2024

## Goal

The goal of this series of workshops is to explore the future of the Deep Funding Reputation System.

## Context

The Reputation-Focus Group initiative was launched during the 3rd round of Deep Funding, thanks to a successful proposal that secured the initial financing. This funding allows the formation of a team tasked with conducting a comprehensive analysis of the existing reputation mechanisms within the broader SingularityNET ecosystem. The goal is to discern recurring themes and similarities and explore how these elements, along with new systems, could be integrated into a reputation system tailored for Deep Funding.

To achieve this, the focus group will organize a sequence of online workshops designed to bring together the right mix of individuals, viewpoints, and creative ideas essential for the conceptualization of a DeepFunding-specific reputation system.

## Outcome

The final outcome of the focus group's efforts will be a detailed report summarizing the insights collected, accompanied by a set of recommendations outlining the potential design, development, and implementation strategies for an effective Deep Funding Reputation System.&#x20;

Another possible outcome is the submission of a follow-up proposal that includes a number of further development steps and possible integration into Deep Funding.

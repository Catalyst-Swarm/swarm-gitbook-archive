# 1.4 DFR3 Dework Server

Once your Discord is set up and you joined the SingularityNET Discord Server, you can sign up on the DeepFunding Dework [via this link](https://app.dework.xyz/i/3Vjk410UuT8WRMKjBzg2ql). Dework is a Kanban Board, nicely adapted to community engagements which we use as a background tool to coordinate and track tasks for specific DeepFunding Engagements.&#x20;



**Make sure to sign in with your Discord Account** (no need to connect any wallet to Dework). \
Once you are on the DeepFunding Dework, **make sure to click the blue “follow” button on the top-right corner.** <br>

That's it, you made it.  No further actions are required thus far and we can move ahead.<br>

( And don't worry, you won't have to engage at Dework as a proposers, We use the Kanban Board mainly for review & admin operations which do not require proposers to act on Dework itself )

# 2.3 I can fill out my Milestone details with the necessary information

On the Contract page Number 12, you will find a table where you have to fill in required information about your proposal Milestones. Ideally, you simply copy-paste the Milestone details from your initial Proposal to the document. The required informations include:

* Your Milestone Numbers
* Your Milestone Names
* Your Milestone Deliverables
* Your Milestone Budgets

\
**Milestone Changes:**

We recognise that we act and engage in a fast changing environment and that you may have to do some little tweaks and adjustments to your initial Milestones. You are able to adjust your Milestones as long as the changes do not violate the initial Proposals intent and deliverables.&#x20;

If you change initial Milestone details from your proposal, please use the Table on the top of the Milestones Table to document which changes you want to make as well as a description of the needed changes.&#x20;

Please be short and concise in the rationale of change descriptions.

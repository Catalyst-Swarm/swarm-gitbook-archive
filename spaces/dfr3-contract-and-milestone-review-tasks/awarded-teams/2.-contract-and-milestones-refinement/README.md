### **SLT  201 I have received my contract draft**

You receive an email which includes the link to your contract draft in the form of a Google Document. Check your email inbox, if you can't find the email there, you may have to check your spam folder.



***

<br>

### **SLT  202 I can fill out the contract with the necessary information**

When opening the Contract, you will notice that you have permissions to add “suggestions” to the document, this is how you can add the required information to the contract. Take some time and read carefully through the document.

Some parts of the contract are marked with colours, mainly with “yellow” and “blue” marks, these colour fields highlight where your attention and input is required.

**Blue colour marks:** Highlight informations you definitely should be aware of\
**Yellow colour marks:** Highlight the fields where you will have to submit your input.



Please fill in any required information in the fields which are marked in yellow.

<br>

The DeepFunding Community Review Team will check and audit your submitted suggestions and approve or reject them. If the reviewers have questions about your submissions on the document, they will directly comment on your suggestions, making it easy for proposers & reviewers to quickly communicate with each other.

<br>

If everything is in order, the reviewers will accept and resolve your submissions.

If problems arise which require deeper communications, we will open a Discord thread with you, the reviewers and e.g SingularityNET & DeepFunding Internal Staff to further advice and facilitate changes. More on that in “SLT 204”.

<br>

***



### **SLT  203 I can fill out my Milestone details with the necessary information**

<br>

On the Contract page Number 12, you will find a table where you have to fill in required information about your proposal Milestones. Ideally, you simply copy-paste the Milestone details from your initial Proposal to the document. The required informations include:

<br>

* Your Milestone Numbers
* Your Milestone Names
* Your Milestone Deliverables
* Your Milestone Budgets

\
Milestone Changes:

We recognise that we act and engage in a fast changing environment and that you may have to do some little tweaks and adjustments to your initial Milestones. You are able to adjust your Milestones as long as the changes do not violate the initial Proposals intent and deliverables. If you change initial Milestone details from your proposal, please use the Table on the top of the Milestones Table to document which changes you want to make as well as a description of the needed changes. Please be short and concise in the rationale of change descriptions.



***



### **SLT  204 I can communicate at Discord with the Review Team**



If your submission on the Contract Document requires deeper communications, the review teams will open a dedicated Thread on the SingularityNET Discord Server where you are able to check in with the Review Teams and E.g SingularityNET & DeepFunding Internal Staff on the raised issues.&#x20;

<br>

***

<br>

#### Note\*

Please keep in mind to engage in a respectful and constructive way with the reviewers and SingularityNET & DeepFunding Internal Staff. We are here to help and to support you on your Proposals implementation.&#x20;

However, as an awarded proposer, you have some responsibilities towards the SingularityNET Ecosystem and Community as whole and you are one of a larger group of awarded teams. DeepFunding is not able to allocate all its attention to you alone as we treat everyone equally, meaning that special wishes or desires from individuals can't always be respected if they move too far from the general formats and processes.

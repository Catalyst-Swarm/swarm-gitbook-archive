# 3.1 I receive, review and sign my final Contract

Once all your submitted suggestions get approved, the Contract will move in its final stage where both parties, the Proposer and the SingularityNET Foundation will sign the Contract.

And this is how it goes:<br>

After approving all your submitted suggestions, you will receive an email of the Contract via DropSign. DropSign is an online signature app which is used in DeepFunding to sign contracts. <br>

After receiving the Contract via DropSign, please:

* Read through the Contract
* Sign the Contract



And that's it, after you signed the Contract, SingularityNET Foundation will receive a notification and sign the contract from their side.&#x20;



Et voila, we are all set and you are now ready to go in full action on implementing your proposal.

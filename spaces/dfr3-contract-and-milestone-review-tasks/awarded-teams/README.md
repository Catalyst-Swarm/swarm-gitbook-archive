# Awarded Teams

This Course is dedicated to support and guide awarded teams in DeepFunding Round 3 through their contract & Milestone refinement process. The Course consists of 4 Modules, starting from simple getting started towards the areas of building background knowledge and contribution.

Each Module entails several Student Learning Targets (SLT) which outline the specific steps which need to be done by the awarded teams in DeepFunding Round 3.<br>

Here a high level overview on the Modules and Targets:

####

#### Module 100 Getting Started

SLT 101 I can create a Discord Account

SLT 102 I can create a Cardano Wallet

SLT 103 I know how to join the SingularityNET Discord Server

SLT 104 I know how to sign in at the DFR3 Dework Server

<br>

***

#### Module 200 Contract & Milestones Refinement

SLT  201 I have received my contract draft

SLT  202 I can fill out the contract with the necessary information

SLT  203 I can fill out my Milestone details with the necessary information

SLT  204 I can communicate at Discord with the Review Team

<br>

***

#### Module 300 Contract & Milestone Signatures

SLT 301 I can receive, review and sign my final Contract

SLT 302 I can send feedback for Contracts used at the next DF round

<br>

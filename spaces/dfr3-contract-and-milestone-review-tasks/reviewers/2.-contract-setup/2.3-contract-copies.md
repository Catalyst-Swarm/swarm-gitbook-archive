# 2.3 Contract Copies

Create a copy of the Milestone and Contract template using the "Deep Funding Contract R3-\[Pool]-\[#]" as the doc’s title. Here is the \[Pool]-\[#] list:[ https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11DCzFuf7wvTj6u7SOWuphI1llwn-\_af73BSSGLAmx-w/edit?usp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11DCzFuf7wvTj6u7SOWuphI1llwn-_af73BSSGLAmx-w/edit?usp=sharing)

<br>

To Create your copy, follow this step by step guide :)

1\) Open The Document, and you should see this:

<figure><img src="https://3471447668-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FofpTfoiRx2YdqV5BMbRD%2Fuploads%2F0XBADTzJRSvySaB1u5ZD%2F1.png?alt=media&#x26;token=4a33224d-129c-4bb1-a50c-fd038d2edc2c" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on Files on the top left corner and you see a menu poping up

<figure><img src="https://3471447668-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FofpTfoiRx2YdqV5BMbRD%2Fuploads%2FRdatNLHfb2gJYqEDPh17%2F2.png?alt=media&#x26;token=830b81b1-a0e2-4274-8c34-2bf6e336c8f5" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. now, click the "Make a copy" item

<figure><img src="https://3471447668-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FofpTfoiRx2YdqV5BMbRD%2Fuploads%2F4W9N5TmtDtdxEcgwTtYH%2F3.png?alt=media&#x26;token=b2fb1109-41e4-40a0-af1a-54332a18fecf" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\) Fill in the Proposal ID as Document title and also click the "copy comments and suggestions" button. ([ You can find the Proposal IDs at this sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11DCzFuf7wvTj6u7SOWuphI1llwn-_af73BSSGLAmx-w/edit#gid=0) )

Finally click "Make a Copy"

<figure><img src="https://3471447668-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FofpTfoiRx2YdqV5BMbRD%2Fuploads%2F6whhekuUy1fwiPUe9PiT%2F4.png?alt=media&#x26;token=9d28c273-90c0-4cd5-bf44-97272199af8a" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Et Voila, you now created a copy and we can move forward to 2.4 Google Folders\

# 2.4 Google Folders

To ensure that the contract documents are effectively stored and moved DeepFunding Round 3 uses google documents and folders. There are 3 Folders.

#### 1) Folder: To be Sent

This is the folder where we save all contract copies made by the reviewers

#### 2) Folder: Sent

Once the contract copies are sent and filled out by the awarded teams, the contract copies get transformed into PDF files and uploaded on the “Sent” folder

#### 3) Folder: Archive

Once the Contracts are signed from both parties, the proposer and the SingularityNET Foundation, the signed contracts get moved into “Archive”, marking the final part of the contract process.

\

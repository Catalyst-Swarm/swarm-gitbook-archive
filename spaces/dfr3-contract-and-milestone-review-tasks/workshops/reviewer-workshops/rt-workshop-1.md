### Workshop Summary

Presenting major priorities of the current approach towards task based processes and to highlight that current processes will be constant subject of change as DeepFunding will evolve as whole and any processes will have to be adjusted and further developed over future DeepFunding Rounds.

Main objective is to nurture a strongly engaged and educated community which engages not only as participants but as DeepFunding operators, ensuring that we build the needed knowledge and capacity within the DeepFunding Community to open up and decentralised administrative processes and support DeepFundings evolution towards a DAO.

Further, presenting the current end-to-end process and required engagements from the Review Teams to guide, assess and audit contract and milestone refinements by awarded Teams in DeepFunding Round 3.

<br>

### **Workshop Outcomes**

\
Adjusted processes to ease the engagements for all sides, Awarded Teams, Review Teams & SNET/DF Internal Staff.

* Review Teams will fill in required information on Contract Page 12 at the Milestone Header, including: Proposal ID, Portal Link, Total Budget
* DF internal staff will create all Contract Copies for awarded Teams in DFR3 as well as corresponding Folders
* Contact to Awarded Teams will mainly happen via email,&#x20;
* Communications on Contract details from the Awarded Teams will happen on the Google Document itself via Suggestions (by the Awarded Teams) and Comments (by the Review Teams). Discord only used in the case that communication on the document itself
* Dework will be only used to track and document Contract progress and will be used only by Review Team Coordinators and SNET/DF internal Staff

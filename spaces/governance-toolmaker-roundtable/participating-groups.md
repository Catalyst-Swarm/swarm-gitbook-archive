# Participating Groups



### [🌐](https://cardanofoundation.org/en/)Cardano Foundation&#x20;

The Cardano Foundation is the independent, Swiss-based and non-profit organization responsible for stewarding the advancement of the public, permissionless blockchain Cardano.

### [🌐](https://cardano.org/ambassadors/)Cardano Ambassadors&#x20;

The Cardano Ambassador program is designed to promote awareness and educate the wider community to drive the adoption of Cardano for better use cases.

Due to strong community insights and perspectives, the participation from Cardano Ambassadors helps to understand high-level pictures, visions, needs and challenges of the Cardano Communtiy.

### [🌐](https://catalystswarm.com/)Catalyst Swarm

The Catalyst Swarm is a global assembly of highly engaged Community members who collectively explore the future of decentralized infrastructure, governance and society.

The Catalyst Swarm initiated the Governance Toolmaker Roundtable and ensures that collective success is achieved by collaboration on common community needs and visions in regard to future emergence and development of Cardano Governance

### [🌐](https://dripdropz.io/)Dripdropz&#x20;

DripDropz provides token dispensing services to the Cardano community. An intuitive platform that offers projects a comprehensive selection of distribution parameters.

DripDropz and the Team gathered serious on-chain voting mechanisms and processes on the Cardano Blockchain and joins the group to share perspectives, experience and expertise.

### [🌐](https://www.lidonation.com/en)Lidonation

Blockchain education in plain English, Kiswahili & Español, outreach, and global community-building: because the future is for everyone.

### [🌐](https://www.photrek.io/home)Photrek

_Photrek_ seeks to _Enlighten the Pathway_ for its customers through the development of machine intelligence capabilities that simplify complex challenges. The values of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) concerns are not just principles for _Photrek’s_ operation, they are core capabilities offered to their clients

### [🌐](https://www.snapbrillia.com/)Snapbrillia&#x20;

Snapbrillia is an inclusive hiring system that creates fair opportunities and empowers your teams to hire the best people faster regardless of who they are. We provide diversity analytics and an unbiased hiring platform for enterprise businesses and fast-growing startups.

Further, Snapbrillia develops on-chain and open sourced voting mechanisms on the Cardano Blockchain with focus on quadratic voting.

Lidonation developed a very first community led all-in-one landing page for Project Catalyst and built serious capacity in regard of analytics and education

### [🌐](https://voteaire.io/)Voteaire&#x20;

Voteaire allows everyone in the ecosystem to create a poll. All results are weighted. All proposals and votes are stored publicly on-chain.

The strong experience and expertise from the Voteaire Team helps to identify and capture major topics and potential directions on Voting tooling, processes and platform requirements.

# Cardano Community | Governance Workshops

**Introduction:**

This Cardano Community Governance Workshops is a proposed framework for community-led governance on the Cardano blockchain. The goal of this framework is to create a bottom-up approach for decision-making within the Cardano community, where stakeholders can actively participate in the direction and development of the network.

The framework is designed to be iterative, with workshops structured in quarterly cycles. This allows for continuous improvement and evolution based on the feedback and expertise gained during each cycle. The retrospective aspect of the design is crucial for ensuring that improvements are made and that the process remains community-led.

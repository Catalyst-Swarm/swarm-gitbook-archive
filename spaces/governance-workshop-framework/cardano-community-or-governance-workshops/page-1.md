**Introduction:**

The Cardano Community Governance Workshops is a proposed framework for community-led governance on the Cardano blockchain. The goal of this framework is to create a bottom-up approach for decision-making within the Cardano community, where stakeholders can actively participate in the direction and development of the network.

The framework is designed to be iterative, with workshops structured in quarterly cycles. This allows for continuous improvement and evolution based on the feedback and expertise gained during each cycle. The retrospective aspect of the design is crucial for ensuring that improvements are made and that the process remains community-led.

**Objectives**

* To create a bottom-up approach to support Cardano community led Governance engagements within the ecosystem
* To provide a platform for stakeholders to actively participate in the direction and development of the network
* To improve expertise, knowledge, and know-how within the community to tackle upcoming challenges
* To ensure that the process remains community-led and continuously improves over time

**Process**

The Cardano Community Governance Workshops will be structured in quarterly cycles, with each cycle consisting of three phases:

1. Preparation: During this phase, the community will discuss and identify governance topics that need to be addressed.
2. Workshop: During this phase, stakeholders will come together to discuss and develop solutions for the identified governance topics.
3. Retrospective: During this phase, the community will evaluate the outcomes of the workshop and identify areas for improvement.

**Expected Outcomes**

* Increased participation and engagement within the Cardano community
* Improved decision-making process based on community feedback and expertise
* Development of solutions for important governance topics
* Continuous improvement and evolution of the framework based on retrospective feedback<br>

**Organising**

When it comes to organizing the in-person workshops, it will be done by the community members themselves, similar to the Cardano Summit and its community-led statelit events all around the globe. The idea is to have local leaders and community members take ownership of the workshops in their respective regions. These leaders will be responsible for organizing the venue, arranging for the necessary equipment, and inviting relevant stakeholders and their communities to participate in the workshops.

To ensure that the workshops are conducted in a structured and efficient manner, a detailed guide and checklist will be collaboratively set up with local communities. This guide will include information on the agenda, the format of the workshop, and the expected outcomes.

Overall, the in-person workshops will be a collaborative effort across the global Cardano community and local community maintainers. By working together, we can ensure that the workshops are conducted in a professional and effective manner, and that the desired outcomes are achieved.





We propose the implementation of the Cardano Community Governance Workshops as a framework to support community-led governance on the Cardano blockchain. This framework will provide an additional platform for stakeholders to actively participate in the direction and development of the network, while ensuring that the process remains community-led and continuously improves over time.

We believe that this framework will lead to increased participation and engagement within the Cardano community, improved decision-making based on community feedback and expertise, and the development of solutions for important governance topics.

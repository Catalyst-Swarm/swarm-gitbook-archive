# FUND 9

Our proposal and idea in this fund is to allow Dreps to achieve saturation (1% of the total stake). To achieve this goal the LATAM Cardano Community will provide campaigning funds and an office, so their voices and platforms can be heard abroad, reaching whales and main countries. This was an idea voted on and supported by the community. It has a budget of USD $19,700.



### Candidates

In a first step to execute this idea. A public registry was opened for all those who want to be Dreps to register. The form closed on December 8, 2022. These were the registered candidates.

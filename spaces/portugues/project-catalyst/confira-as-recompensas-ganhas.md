# Confira as recompensas ganhas

O seguinte ajuda você a verificar as recompensas recebidas do Project Catalyst.

1. Abra a guia Recompensas no Daedalus e copie sua chave de aposta.

![](https://300765841-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0J9oMrSu1VHXR0BqdmZJ%2Fuploads%2FElJ9mE4w9iCR0KSAihFw%2Frewards1.png?alt=media\&token=42e818e6-5713-4419-802b-b71c6216c753)

1. Abra o [Cardanoscan](https://cardanoscan.io) e cole sua chave de aposta e clique em **Pesquisar**

![](https://300765841-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0J9oMrSu1VHXR0BqdmZJ%2Fuploads%2Fejh9z3sF6S972daENHFJ%2Frewards2.png?alt=media\&token=06510f7f-97f1-4181-8376-25734ab19f8f)

3\. Clique em **Recompensas Instantâneas**

![](https://300765841-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0J9oMrSu1VHXR0BqdmZJ%2Fuploads%2Feeoxal5lSQpnHUT7ms4y%2Frewards3.png?alt=media\&token=c80aaed9-c54c-407d-8b80-f4c6e3a389a0)

1. Você pode ver o último saldo de recompensas distribuídas aqui:

![](https://300765841-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0J9oMrSu1VHXR0BqdmZJ%2Fuploads%2FXm9gAt188duiEqYgCa9b%2Frewards4.png?alt=media\&token=6ab9a295-2e00-4267-82c0-6c5f7b3dd24f)

# Ambassadors program

As highly engaged and committed members, we engage since early 2022 in the SingularityNET Ambassador program, where we introduce and implement collaboratively tools and processes such as public and transparent project management at the Ambassadors Dework, treasury & accounting systems to recognize community engagements and the initiation of Ambassador Guilds, such as the Marketing & Treasury Guild&#x20;

Check the established working Spaces where Ambassadors work on tasks, and where the wider community can apply to rewarded Bounties.

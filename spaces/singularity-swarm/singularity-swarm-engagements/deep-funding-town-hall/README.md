# Deep Funding Town Hall

In late 2022, we organised and hosted a very first DeepFunding Town Hall, in early 2023 we organised and hosted follow up events. The Deep Funding Town Hall | Proposal Reels events on February 2nd and February 9th invited Deep Funding proposers to present their proposals to the community and to have an open community space for discussions and questions around all things DeepFunding.

# Governance Expert Group

the Swarm participates constantly in the Deep Funding Governance Expert group led by the SingularityNET Foundation. We introduced Catalyst & Cardano community members with strong experience at Cardano & Catalyst to the initiative which enabled us to provide high quality feedback and insights on what worked well, and what didn't at project Catalyst. These insights help to avoid duplications of mistakes and efforts at the emerging Deep Funding project and also help to experiment, explore and bootstrap effective alternatives and improvements.

# 1.1. Governance Framework Purpose

[**Open to Edit Document**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PFlghur2A-qXsu7h6CxxhImQIcPXQ8YnaUCcR1kdgUo/edit?usp=sharing)<br>

The purpose of this document is to serve as the starting point from which the SingularityNet Ambassador community begins its decentralized governance journey. The development of this  framework guides activities, initiatives, and behaviours of ambassadors while allowing room for innovation and different ways of knowing. It should act as a guidepost for the community, or for others seeking similar objectives.&#x20;

Importantly, the SingularityNet Ambassadors Governance Framework itself should be seen as an evergreen document, which means that the text you read now may change as new people join the process and bring with them diverse views and opinions on what is captured here.&#x20;

Those reading this document should consider themselves as active participants in its development and maintenance. All views are welcome and encouraged. If you have views on improving this document, please consider the following process:

1. Read first&#x20;
2. Then think
3. Then think some more
4. Then act
5. Repeat as needed

SingularityNet Ambassadors should be  open, inclusive, and transparent, both in words and in actions.  Everyone who participates should feel comfortable sharing ideas and working together to create an environment that’s welcoming and delivers a sense of pride in advancing  our shared objectives.&#x20;

Ultimately, the Framework seeks to demonstrate the potential benefits of, and establish a solid foundation for, the SingularityNET Ambassadors program as it continues to evolve and grow within an decentralized and on-chain AI ecosystem.&#x20;

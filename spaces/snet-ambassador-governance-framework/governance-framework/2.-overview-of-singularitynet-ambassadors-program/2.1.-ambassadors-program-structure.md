# 2.1. Ambassadors Program Structure

[**Open to edit Document**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1znMqN_tB1LUYkwddbrNOt7tGNJRIcRkaCTSt4DmapA8/edit?usp=sharing)

At the core of the Ambassadors program you will find the individuals dedicated to specific projects and activities within the program but all working towards our shared objectives. Individuals participate directly or indirectly via the formation of workgroups and guilds, around common interests and objectives by creating spaces for them to collaborate on tasks, share ideas, and collectively drive the program forward.

Ambassadors fulfill various roles within the program including but not limited to: content creation, translation services, social media moderation, event organization, and technical support.&#x20;

Ambassadors are also encouraged to identify other areas within the ecosystem where their skills can be employed, even in areas which have not yet been identified. The flexibility of these roles enables community members to contribute in ways that align with their unique skills, interests and capabilities, while fostering a dynamic and inclusive environment across the program.

Our bottom-up approach to governance and program organization is intended to keep the Ambassadors program agile, responsive, and adaptive to the ever-evolving landscape of decentralized AI and blockchain technology.

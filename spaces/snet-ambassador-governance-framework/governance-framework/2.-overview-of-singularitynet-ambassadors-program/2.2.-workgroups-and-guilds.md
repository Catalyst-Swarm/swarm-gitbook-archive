# 2.2. Workgroups and Guilds

[**Open to edit Document**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Ndr7ceK6IrVNXKZsp61a3KrbOcKjcAiSeZO1VSJ8nJc/edit?usp=sharing)

Workgroups and guilds of the SingularityNET Ambassadors program are essential for facilitating collaboration and specialization across the ecosystem. Each group brings together ambassadors and stakeholders with specific skills and interests and enables them to work effectively on projects and activities that align with their expertise. Some of the current guilds within the program include:



1. Marketing Guild: The Marketing Guild is dedicated to building awareness of SingularityNET and its ecosystem by creating and distributing content, promoting events, and engaging with the community on various social media platforms. Ambassadors in this guild contribute to outreach efforts, public relations, and branding strategies to enhance the visibility and understanding of SingularityNET's mission and goals.
2. Treasury Guild: The Treasury Guild focuses on the financial operations, funds movement, and token distributions within the Ambassadors program and ensures transparency, and accountability related to the program's financial activities. Ambassadors in the Treasury Guild play a crucial role in managing resources and maintaining the financial health of the program.
3. Process Guild: The Process Guild is responsible for developing and improving the processes and operations of the Ambassadors program by identifying areas for improvement, streamlining workflows, and implementing best practices that enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of the program. Ambassadors in the Process Guild contribute their expertise in project management, process optimization, and quality assurance to support the evolution of the program.
4. Strategy Guild: The Strategy Guild focuses on building and refining the overall strategy for the Ambassadors program by setting goals, identifying opportunities, and planning initiatives that align with the program's mission and the broader objectives of the SingularityNET ecosystem. Ambassadors in the Strategy Guild contribute their strategic thinking, foresight, and analytical skills to ensure the program's continued growth and success.



These guilds represent just a few examples of the diverse efforts within the SingularityNET Ambassadors program as each plays a vital role in advancing broader ecosystem objectives.  New guilds may be established to address emerging needs and opportunities so that the program remains dynamic and responsive to the ever-changing landscape of decentralized AI and blockchain technology.
